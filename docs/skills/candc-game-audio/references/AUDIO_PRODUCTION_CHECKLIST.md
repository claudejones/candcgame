# Audio Production Checklist

## Before generation
- Confirm asset ID/path.
- Confirm it is not already approved.
- Verify stage facts when stage-specific.
- Select Advanced v6 / Sounds One Shot / Sounds Loop.
- Verify Sounds prompt ≤500 chars.

## Stage music QA
- Instrumental.
- Consistent C&C 8-bit/chiptune identity.
- Distinct stage identity.
- Immediate useful opening.
- Strong side-scrolling momentum.
- No distracting vocals/long intro/slow breakdown.
- Enough development for ~90 sec.
- Does not sound like a crude stereotype.
- WAV master downloaded after approval.

## SFX QA
- Event is immediately readable.
- Short and non-fatiguing.
- Works over music.
- No unnecessary reverb/ambience.
- Similar SFX remain distinguishable.
- WAV master downloaded after approval.

## Loop QA
- Seam is not obvious.
- Repetition is tolerable.
- Does not mask BGM/gameplay.
- Loop length suits its role.

## Stinger QA
- Celebration/state change is immediate.
- Strong resolution.
- Duration does not delay progression.
- Transition from BGM feels intentional.

## Integration QA later
- Normalize perceived loudness by category.
- Music duck/fade behavior tested.
- SFX concurrency tested.
- Pause/mute/settings behavior tested.
- Mobile browser playback tested.
- 60/120 Hz gameplay unaffected.
