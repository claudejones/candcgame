## 3. Heart ratings, trophies and passport rules

### Confirmed rules

1. Each of the 21 stages has a unique trophy design shared across difficulties.
2. Completing a stage with one, two or three hearts awards that trophy at that rating. Three filled hearts represents a perfect run under the user’s current wording.
3. A better replay upgrades the saved rating; a worse replay must never downgrade it.
4. Completing all three stages in a continent earns its regular passport stamp.
5. Perfect trophies on all three stages earn the continent’s perfect passport stamp.
6. Easy, Standard and Hard each maintain their own achievement records and views.
7. Uncompleted trophies and stamps are visible in gray. Earned versions become colored.
8. All seven perfect continent stamps in **Standard** unlock a special level, currently undesigned. Show a mystery tile before it unlocks.
9. Unlimited-health play is not eligible for achievements, and the player must be told.

### Recommended result calculation

For each eligible successful stage completion, store:

`bestHearts[difficulty][stageId] = max(previousBest, heartsRemainingAtFinish)`

Use zero for unearned; successful ratings are 1–3. Calculate the stamp from the stage records rather than maintaining a second, potentially contradictory counter:

- Regular stamp: all three stage ratings are at least 1.
- Perfect stamp: all three stage ratings equal 3.
- Special-level entitlement: all 21 Standard stage ratings equal 3.

This permits separate improved replays to complete a perfect continent; it does not require one uninterrupted three-stage or world run. The stamp upgrades in place with a gold border/star and “Perfect” label. Its silhouette should remain recognizable without color.

Examples: NA01 Standard 1 → 3 upgrades the trophy; a later 2 leaves the saved best at 3. NA01 Easy 3 does not upgrade NA01 Standard. NA01/02/03 Standard ratings 3/3/2 give a regular stamp; improving NA03 to 3 gives the perfect stamp. Repeated completion grants no duplicate trophy or notification.

### Health and hearts — clarified

**Confirmed:** health and lives are the same three-heart meter; there is no separate stock of attempts called lives. Losing all three hearts ends the current attempt and shows Game Over. Unlimited Health prevents hearts from being depleted. Assisted stage completions earn no trophy, rating improvement, passport or permanent unlock. Preserve already-earned legitimate records.

Use **Health** for the meter and **Unlimited Health** for the option. Workbench must preserve or explicitly resolve hit reactions and collision feedback under assistance; do not assume disabling heart loss also disables collision detection.

**Confirmed:** every stage and every retry starts with three hearts; health does not carry over from the previous stage. There is no separate lives counter. Three hearts at finish means perfect under the current request; if health can refill, decide whether perfect also requires zero damage. No healing system is approved by this plan. Workbench must define finish-versus-fatal-hit ordering so the same attempt cannot both fail and earn a trophy.

## 5. Saving and Continue

Recommend autosaving settings when changed and progress after every committed result, unlock and map arrival. A subtle “Saved on this device” message plus a Save Progress action on the map makes saving discoverable. Manual Save and autosave use the same operation. If a save fails, keep the previous save and report the failure; never display a false success.

Recommend one journey checkpoint per difficulty and a remembered most-recent journey for Continue. Store checkpoint stage, chosen character and assistance state alongside separate persistent best-result and entitlement records. Before overwriting a checkpoint, explain the effect. Switching startup difficulty does not erase another journey.

For initial scope, Continue restarts the unfinished stage from its beginning; it does not restore exact positions, obstacles or mid-jump state. Completed-stage rewards must be saved before the results animation, so closing the app during a celebration does not lose them or award them twice on reopen. Retry abandons an attempt without lowering stored results.

Player saves are local to the browser/device/origin. Changing hosts, clearing browser data or private sessions may make them unavailable. Do not promise cloud sync or guaranteed permanent storage. Workbench project exports/settings are separate from player saves. Demo, Design and Test sessions must not write production achievements. Export/import of player progress is an optional later feature, not a launch requirement in this draft.

The route’s visual placement can be stylized, but use stage IDs and order supplied by the stage catalog. Keep all 21 logical stage locations and hit targets outside the painted base image.

Use the same mechanism for continent travel with a modest change in duration/zoom; no airplane/boat artwork is required unless later chosen.

Recommended result sequence:

1. Freeze the final attempt outcome and commit eligible progress once.
2. Show the results panel with this-attempt hearts and previous/current best.
3. Reveal a first trophy or upgrade only if something improved.
4. For a newly complete/perfect continent, show the stamp as the next card in the same panel. Combine simultaneous awards rather than stacking unrelated dialogs.
5. Continue returns to the map, advances the marker, reveals the next available stage and offers Play.

Recommend short 0.6–1.0 second trophy/stamp reveals and 1–2 second travel. Tap skips decorative motion while preserving the result; reduced-motion preference uses an immediate state change or short fade. Buttons become available promptly, and repeated taps must not replay awards or launch a stage twice. “Stage Complete”, “New Best” and “Passport Earned” are runtime text, not baked into images.

Optional attract demo: after 10 seconds idle only on the root Start screen, play roughly 15–20 seconds of NA01, muted by default, labeled “Demo — tap to return”, then return to Start. Never trigger while a submenu/dialog is open, an input is focused, or the page is hidden. Demo has isolated state, no rewards, no save changes and no automatic music override. Recommend postponing until core screens work.
